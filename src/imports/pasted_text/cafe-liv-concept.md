# DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a sophisticated **Swiss Alpine hospitality aesthetic**.

The visual style should combine:

**Swiss editorial design + Alpine nature + premium independent café**

Use:

* warm ivory / cream backgrounds
* natural beige and stone tones
* muted alpine green
* charcoal typography
* subtle brown/earth accents
* elegant serif typography for major headings
* clean modern sans-serif for body text
* large immersive photography
* generous whitespace
* subtle borders
* tasteful shadows
* restrained rounded corners
* subtle hover animations
* smooth scrolling

The design should feel:

**Warm. Authentic. Premium. Calm. Swiss.**

Avoid:

* neon colors
* excessive gradients
* glassmorphism
* excessive rounded cards
* generic SaaS layouts
* excessive animations
* huge blocks of text
* fake statistics
* fake awards
* fake testimonials
* fake menu prices
* artificial-looking stock imagery
